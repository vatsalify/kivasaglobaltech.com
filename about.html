About Kivasa

Lighting the backbone of infrastructure

Reliable. Technical. Progressive. An infrastructure-focused lighting and electrical partner for the projects that keep India moving.

Kivasa Globaltech Pvt Ltd provides lighting and electrical solutions for infrastructure projects across Railways, commercial and residential developments, and industry.

From railway stations to commercial complexes and industrial plants, we deliver reliable, efficient electrical solutions. With direct OEM partnerships and authentic sourcing, we work with contractors, infrastructure companies, procurement heads, real estate groups and government contracting companies.

We know what goes wrong in this industry: delays, poor quality, poor communication, over-commitment and under-delivery. Kivasa exists to be the partner that doesn't do those things.

Our mission

To empower Indian manufacturers by showcasing their products to a wider audience, at home and abroad, and to be a trusted partner for seamless market entry and growth.

Our vision

To become the best electrical supply and works company in the nation, setting new benchmarks in quality, reliability and execution.
