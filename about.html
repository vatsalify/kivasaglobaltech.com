Our Story & Purpose

Building India's Infrastructure, One Landmark at a Time

Kivasa Globaltech Pvt Ltd is a specialized, infrastructure-focused solutions provider delivering premium lighting and electrical installations for India's most critical public and private sector projects. Headquartered in Vadodara, Gujarat, we operate with a dedicated nation-building mindset, serving Railways, government contracting companies, EPC developers, and industrial manufacturers.

Our approach is grounded in three core principles: Reliable • Technical • Progressive. We speak confidently, clearly, and practically — prioritizing engineered solutions, verified photometric compliance, and strict timeline adherence above all else.

In an industry often challenged by delayed turnaround times, opaque pricing, and nonexistent after-sales support, Kivasa Globaltech stands as a dependable single-window partner. Through direct OEM partnerships with industry leaders like Jaquar, we ensure certified product authenticity, project-specific pricing, and prompt on-site execution.

Our Mission

To supply, engineer, and execute high-performance lighting and electrical infrastructure for Indian Railways, industrial facilities, and commercial developments — delivering uncompromised quality, verified energy efficiency, and strict timeline adherence on every project.

Our Vision

To become India's foremost electrical supply and infrastructure execution company, establishing national benchmarks for engineering excellence, OEM transparency, and sustainable energy stewardship across all public and industrial works.
