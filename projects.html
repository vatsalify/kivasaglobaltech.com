Mehsana Railway Station

Mehsana's redeveloped station needed a facade that reads clearly from the city side at night. We supplied the facade lighting that now outlines the full length of the building and its screens.

The result: improved visibility and aesthetics across the station premises, and a building that works as a landmark after dark.
